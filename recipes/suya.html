Suya

Description

Suya is a popular Nigerian street snack made from grilled meat, it could be beef or chicken.

Ingredients:

  • 500g beef or chicken, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ground peanuts
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. In a bowl, mix the ground peanuts, paprika, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt.
  2. Coat the sliced meat with the spice mixture and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat a grill or barbecue to medium-high heat.
  4. Thread the marinated meat onto skewers.
  5. Grill the skewers for about 5-7 minutes on each side or until the meat is cooked through and slightly charred.
  6. Serve hot with sliced onions and tomatoes.
