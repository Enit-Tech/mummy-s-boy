Amala and Ewedu

Description

Amala and Ewedu is a traditional Nigerian dish made from yam flour (amala) and jute leaves (ewedu).

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups yam flour
  • 1 cup jute leaves, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. In a pot, bring water to a boil and gradually add the yam flour, stirring continuously to avoid lumps. Cook until smooth and stretchy.
  2. In another pot, heat the vegetable oil and sauté the chopped onion and minced garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the chopped jute leaves to the pot and cook for about 5 minutes until tender.
  4. Season with salt to taste.
  5. Serve the amala with the ewedu soup on the side.
