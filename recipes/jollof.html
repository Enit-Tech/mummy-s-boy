JOLLOF RICE

Description

Jollof rice is a popular dish we all love most especially in Nigeria.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of long grain rice
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps:

  1. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion and cook until translucent.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down.
  4. Stir in the curry powder and thyme.
  5. Add the rice and stir to coat with the spice mixture.
  6. Gradually add water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer covered for 20 minutes.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.
